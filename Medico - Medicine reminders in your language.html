Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

Is my caregiver contacted automatically?

No. A message is prepared only if you gave consent, and you press send yourself.

Does Medico suggest doses?

Never. It only repeats what is written on your prescription.

Why can't I hear the Hindi voice?

Your device needs a Hindi text-to-speech voice installed. Add one in system settings, then test it under Voice & settings.

Do reminders work when the page is closed?

No. Voice reminders work while this page is open in your browser.
